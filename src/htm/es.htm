Es es desert vast et vac in hemisfer boreal de Dinaisth.

Plan de Es, mot per vents fort, coper un ret vast de structur non ut. Profund in bacin deg Paradichlorisse, et Helianth, un gener de alg qui emit lumin et cresc in scal simil terass qui descend in profund de Dinaisth.

The wind-swept plains cover a vast network of disused structures. Sunken deep within the basin resides Paradichlorisse, and the Sunflowers a sort of light-emitting algae that grows on terasse-like steps that descend into the depths of Dinaisth.
