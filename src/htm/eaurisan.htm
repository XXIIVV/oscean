The Eaurisan coast extends into the Kanikule distortion. It is a place famous for having an extra season, and in some places nearest to the edge of the distortion, one must walk around any one point in space for one and half turn to complete a full orbit, making the place extremely dangerous but equally valuable to Soies research.

The corridor between Laeisth and Dilitriel is best crossed by glider since the terrain is dotted with chasms. Sometimes written as Eaurison.
