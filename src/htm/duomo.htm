Duomo coper septentrion de Dinaisth.

Duomo e posit septentrion de Es et Laeisth, il hospit sol indigen habitats qui e exist ab advent de Neon Hermetists. Aud capitul de Duomic.

Duomo is located north of Es and Laeisth, it hosts the only remains of indigenous constructions from the earlier times before the arrival of Neon Hermetists. Listen to the Duomic chapters.
