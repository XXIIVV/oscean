Bouc is a file browser and viewer.

A little firmware designed to display plain-text and 1-bit images, nothing more. Not having a smartphone or a printer, sometimes I need to take tickets and receipts with me, so I wanted a way to do so with the Xte Ink but didn't like the stock and community firmwares.

So I made one! It reads icn and txt files from a SD Card.
It also remembers your page.

Controls

  • Up/Down, selection
  • A, open
  • B, back
  • Left, first-page

Wallpaper

The firmware expects an image named wallpaper3cx64 in the root.
