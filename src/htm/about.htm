Seeing yourself blink.

Launched in 2008, this wiki was created to serve as a personal assistant for managing a vast collection of bi-monthly updates, journal logs, project notes, and curated pages of general knowledge.

This wiki is implemented in Uxntal which has, as its only dependency, a small virtual machine built to endure the accelerating pace of digital information decay. It is designed to evolve with my needs and adapt to the shifting landscape of modern technology.

Some pages are written in a dialect of Latin devised by the mathematician Giuseppe Peano. It should be possible for English speakers to make sense of the text with very little practice. Alternatively, choose the (Lø) button in the footer to translate the page.

Put un aparat mechanic de futur pro amelior memor de se, un bibliothec privat que se consul veloc et qui retin omn librar, record, epistol, etc. Consider a future device, a sort of mechanized private library in which an individual stores all his books, records, and communications, and which may be consulted with exceeding speed and flexibility. It is an enlarged intimate supplement to his memory. Vannevar Bush, As We May Think
