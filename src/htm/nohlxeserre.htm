Nohlxeserre is a language from the Neauismetica.

Nohlxeserre is a powerful hallucinogenic toxin emitted by Paradichlorisse that delivers a richer and more precise vision than actual experience, it is the language of Actors.

The richness and detail of experiencing an event, pales in comparison to having it recited by Actors. It effectively induces the symptoms of neausea, stasis, and ultimately death, as the Occurring itself thereafter feels like an hallucination.

Andes, as opposed to Neonev, did not arrive on dinaisth looking for Actors, but for Paradichlorisse, whom is expected, at the very end of times, to say the true word for "silence".
