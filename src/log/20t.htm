Tall Grass

By the overgrown orchard, we're doing cartwheels in the tall grass, the summer din gone along with the holidayers returning to reality, the entire island is ours, every crooked apple tree, every shriveled blackberry, every dead leaf; all ours. Endless time in an empty world is our bounty.

Back aboard, the faint smell of smoke stirred by a gust rustling the ashes in the woodstove brings to mind the old fires by the beach, breathing through the collar of a thick sweater as I warm my hands around a cup of tea, and most of all shoegaze.

Autumn is approaching in more ways than one. And still, we'll be doing cartwheels in the tall grass.
