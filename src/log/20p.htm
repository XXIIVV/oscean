il pluv

Weeks now into that summer pace of things, without signal. Forced inside for torrential rain, tossed around by the afternoon wind. Entire days in my bunk with a book and candles wedged between two zincs, there's a bad thing in anything good when there is too much of it. I tell you, even the forest looks sick of it, and I'm tired of reading.

Today, the monotony was momentarily shattered by a boat trying to leave, the fog, the dreariness, I get it, but who then went and beached itself sideways. Eight hours like a dead fish, waiting for the water to return, they've limped back to where they've started and throughout the rain was still going.

At one o'clock, we wander deep in the downpour and through the woods, it's dark as dusk and wet, we can't see where we're stepping it's so dark. We clamber up and down and it's slippery but when it finally opens up, there is black lake. And that water is warm enough for swimming.
