il pluv

Weeks now into that summer pace of things, without signal. Forced inside for torrential rain, tossed around by the afternoon wind. Entire days in my bunk with a book and candles wedged between two zincs, there's even a bad thing in anything good when there is too much of it. I tell you, even the forest looks sick of it, and I'm tired of reading.

The monotony was momentarily shattered by a boat trying to leave, the stinking fog, the dreariness, I can't fucking take it anymore, but who then went and beached itself sideways. I'm so out of here. Eight hours like a dead whale, waiting for the water to return, they've limped back to where they're started and the rain is still going.

At one o'clock, we wander deep into the downpour and through the woods, it's as dark as dusk and wet, we can't see where we're stepping it's so dark. We clamber up and down and it's slippery but when it finally opens up, there is black lake. And that water is warm enough for swimming.
