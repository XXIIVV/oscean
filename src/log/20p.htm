Is it the intrinsic essence of a work that which doesn't have to please and doesn't try to sell. Weeks now into that summer pace of things, without signal. Forced inside for torrential rains, tossed around by afternoon wind. Across the galley, under our respective blankets and through the steam of our respective teacups, we dare not ask each other whys afraid that the spell might shatter.

It's days if you're lucky, months if you're not careful, you chased it all over the world and at last you emerge from its snare, with very little to show for it too. Some ideas are just like that. For a split second you were reminded of that inexpressible nature that sleeps there underneath work.
