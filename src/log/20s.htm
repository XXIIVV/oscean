Husserl "We were less their instrument than pretext,
Lest on themselves the blame they made be turned;
To us instead they point, and authors name."

Derrida "For nought the promise was but th'veil to strip,
That nothing pass unseen between the hands
Of them who each other more than us feared."

Lacan "While concealing the ones that vaults emptied
And blew the flood that drowned their paper gold,
To us instead they point, Monster behold!"

Berkeley "Perceive in us the fall it fears to name,
And never on the ones who lit it look,
The fuse from which at last Rhomb-deau° shall rise."
