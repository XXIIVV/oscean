Aestat deb Termin mox

Aestat deb termin mox, ne ab die plus bref, ne ab tempor plus frigid, sed nam me in fin claud Ulysses et il e es aestat projic de me. Id memor vehement ab Mindgame de Masaaki Yuasa aut Everything Everywhere All At Once. Hoc liber es vald angust et intens, plus dificil que me e expect. Me quas relinq, plur tempor!

Olim, juxt scen ab hospit, dum Oxen Of The Sun, me e pens Fuck it! et jact liber trans navis et reven ad Paradise Lost. Sed tand, me leg Adam Walker's reading notes et tent de nov. Virginia Woolf e od Ulysses in prim, sed in fin incip percip, idem pro me.

Me vol retin exemplar de me, pot es un die, leg hoc caerul liber de nov. Si sol pro fin de Ulysses, forsan maxim bel opus.
